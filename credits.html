Every Window Lit · Data and credits

Season 1 replays real weekly and monthly price moves from public data for Italy, Spain, Germany and the US (Jan 2023 to Dec 2025). The town and everyone in it are fictional. World effects are scaled to each series' 2023–2026 range so they can be seen. Simplified game model, not policy advice.

Each in-game week shows one real period: a week for pump and wholesale power prices, a month for grocery prices. The game ships only the derived numbers: one percentage change per period, how it compares with the series' other changes, the next period's change (to settle the Sunday call) and a few changes before it.

Data

  • Pump prices, Italy. Fonte: Ministero dell'ambiente e della sicurezza energetica – Direzione generale fonti energetiche e titoli abilitativi, prezzi medi settimanali dei carburanti (sisen.mase.gov.it), accessed 31 Oct 2026, licensed CC BY 4.0. Changed: weekly percentage changes of the mean of Euro-super 95 and diesel prices, computed for the game.
  • Pump prices, US. Source: U.S. Energy Information Administration (3 Nov 2026), Weekly U.S. Regular All Formulations Retail Gasoline Prices (eia.gov), accessed 6 Nov 2026, public domain. Changed: weekly percentage changes, computed for the game.
  • Wholesale power prices, Italy, Spain and Germany. Ember, European Wholesale Electricity Price Data (ember-energy.org), accessed 31 Oct 2026, licensed CC BY 4.0; Ember's hourly source is the ENTSO-E Transparency Platform. Changed: weekly averages of daily day-ahead prices and their percentage changes, computed for the game.
  • Grocery prices, Italy. Fonte: Istat, Prezzi al consumo per l'intera collettività (NIC), "beni alimentari, per la cura della casa e della persona" (carrello della spesa), dataflow 167_745_DF_DCSP_NIC1B2025_6 (esploradati.istat.it), accessed 31 Oct 2026, licensed CC BY 4.0. Used as published: percentage change on the previous month, each shown as one in-game week; provisional months left out.
  • Grocery prices, Spain. Source: Eurostat, doi.org/10.2908/PRC_HICP_MINR, accessed 6 Nov 2026: HICP monthly index, food and non-alcoholic beverages, licensed CC BY 4.0. Changed: monthly percentage changes of the index, computed for the game, each shown as one in-game week. Eurostat has no responsibility for these changes or for the game.
  • Grocery prices, US. Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers, food at home, U.S. city average, not seasonally adjusted (bls.gov), accessed 6 Nov 2026, public domain. Changed: monthly percentage changes of the index, computed for the game, each shown as one in-game week; September to December 2025 left out (October was never collected). BLS.gov cannot vouch for the data or analyses derived from these data after the data have been retrieved from BLS.gov.
  • Disaster check, US. This product uses the Federal Emergency Management Agency's OpenFEMA API, but is not endorsed by FEMA. The Federal Government or FEMA cannot vouch for the data or analyses derived from these data after the data have been retrieved from the Agency's website(s). Dataset: FemaWebDisasterDeclarations v1, accessed 6 Nov 2026. Used only to keep the US weeks away from disasters; none of it ships with the game.
  • Weather. The data was obtained from the National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) Langley Research Center (LaRC) Prediction Of Worldwide Energy Resources (POWER) Project funded through the NASA Earth Science Division (power.larc.nasa.gov). Weeks that overlap real disasters

One week is about a real flood on the mainland. The game names the place in a letter and the month on the week's card, shows no damage or injuries, and lets the island respond only with help. It can be switched off on the start page.
